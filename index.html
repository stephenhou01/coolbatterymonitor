Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

App: CoolBatteryMonitor (macOS)

Data Collection

The developer of CoolBatteryMonitor does not collect, receive, or transmit personal data. The app runs entirely on your Mac with no network access.

Permissions

The app requires no special system permissions. It reads battery information through Apple's public IOKit framework APIs, the same APIs used by macOS System Settings.

Third-Party Services

CoolBatteryMonitor does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Storage

The app stores charging history, battery samples, and language preferences only on your Mac in its sandboxed container. This local app data is never sent to the developer or any third party.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page and the "Effective Date" above.
